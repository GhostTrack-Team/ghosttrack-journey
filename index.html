03 — System Architecture
PERCEPTION
INTELLIGENCE
ACTION
Three distinct, modular hardware/software layers that operate in lockstep to provide absolute failsafe redundancy on the edge.
👁️
PERCEPTION
Layer A — Sense
A 160° wide-angle USB camera feeds YOLOv8n running locally at 12–15 FPS on a Raspberry Pi 4, while a co-located ultrasonic sensor takes range telemetry independently.
→
🧠
INTELLIGENCE
Layer B — Decision
An ESP32-S3 microcontroller acts as the central hub. It ingests the AI classification from the Pi, distance data, and blinker indicator, applying a 3-condition logic gate to filter false alarms.
→
⚡
ACTION
Layer C — Feedback
Directional servo actuators physically adjust A-pillar indicators, high-intensity LEDs pulse Stage-1 and Stage-2 visual patterns, and black-box diagnostics write every event to a secure local CSV.

06 — Alert System
TWO-STAGE
SAFETY ALERTS
Real-time indicators mapped to proximity and turning behavior, maximizing A-pillar ambient awareness and physical directional warnings.
1
STAGE 1 — AMBIENT
AWARENESS
A solid Yellow LED illuminates on the corresponding A-pillar dashboard hub. This provides low-cognitive-load spatial awareness, alerting the driver that a road user is alongside them before any lane maneuver is attempted.
TRIGGER: VRU detected in blind spot at any range → Solid Yellow LED ON
2
STAGE 2 — CRITICAL
INTERVENTION
The red LED flashes rapidly, and a physical servo-motor gauge sweeps immediately to point directly at the threat zone. Concurrently, a dual-tone acoustic alarm sound fires through the cabin speaker.
